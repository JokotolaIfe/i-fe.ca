We're I-fe — three engineers who take on a small number of client projects at a time.

I-fe is led by Ifeoluwa Richard, who has spent the past nine years building web and mobile products across banking, retail and logistics — including leading engineering on a banking platform used by more than a million people. Working alongside two collaborators, we cover product engineering, interface design and quality end to end, so a project doesn't stall waiting on a single skill set.

Most projects start the same way: a founder or team with a real problem, and not enough engineering hours to solve it properly. That's where we come in — we take on a small number of projects at a time and see each one through, from architecture to release.

The name is a nod to Ile-Ife, the city Yoruba history points to as the place things began — a fitting starting point for building foundations other people's businesses can run on.
